About Us

Why Choose Sweeto?

12+ Years of Baking

Crafted with Love, Served with Pride

At Sweeto, every dessert is a labour of love. We source only the finest ingredients — Belgian chocolate, organic cream, and locally grown fruits — to create pastries and cakes that are as beautiful as they are delicious.

Our master bakers have honed their craft over 12 years, blending classical French techniques with bold, modern flavour combinations. Whether it's a birthday cake or a casual sweet craving, we have something that will make your day sweeter.
